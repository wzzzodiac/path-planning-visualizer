WHY DO THEY LOOK DIFFERENT?

Three algorithms, three personalities.

A*

Knows the cost already travelled and also estimates how far the goal probably is. Usually the best balance between “correct” and “please stop exploring the entire map”.

Dijkstra

Only cares about total cost from START. It is methodical and guarantees the cheapest route, but it has zero directional intuition and may inspect a lot of cells.

Greedy

Main thought: “goal is over there”. It follows the estimate aggressively and often explores fewer cells, but it can choose a more expensive route because previous travel cost barely matters.
